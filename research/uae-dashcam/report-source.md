# Source and claim ledger: UAE dashcam product decision

**Audience:** student selecting a deployable UAE product track.  
**Research date:** 4 September 2026.  
**Scope:** UAE dashcam market, obtainable hardware, public data/model rights, a practical app architecture, UAE driving-rule references, privacy, and a preliminary IP screen. This is not legal, safety, or patent advice.

## Searches and stopping rationale

Discovery covered official RTA and UAE-law sources, manufacturer/authorised-retailer hardware sources, first-party data/model documentation, UAE competitor product pages, and primary patent records. The evidence converged: real-time coaching and generic risk scoring are existing products; SD-card upload plus post-trip evidence is technically simple and commercially more differentiated; public commercial-ready data exists but must be licence-screened. Further broad market searches did not produce a credible UAE unit-sales or market-size statistic, so none is asserted.
