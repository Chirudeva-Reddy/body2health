# Viable UAE-specific project tracks

Research date: 4 September 2026. These options deliberately avoid body images, clinical inference, hardware, protected personal data, and unverified third-party APIs. They are sized as course projects: an imported official dataset, a small web/data application, and a repeatable evaluation.

## Best three to propose

1. **Dubai public-transport demand explorer and forecast.** This is the strongest data-science track: current, regularly maintained RTA trip data, a clear forecasting task, and no sensitive user data.
2. **Dubai food catalogue search and label explainer.** This is the strongest software/product track: a large official catalogue, useful Arabic/English search, and an easily demonstrated MVP. It must be presented as information retrieval, **not** medical or allergy advice.
3. **Road-safety analytics and campaign recommender.** This is the strongest civic-data track: official accident data by emirate/cause/year supports transparent recommendations without pretending to predict individual risk.

## Options

| Track | UAE relevance and official data | Small MVP | Objective evaluation | Scope risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Dubai public-transport demand explorer and forecast** | RTA publishes a collection of monthly public-transport trip CSVs (bus, metro, tram, marine and rail), with `YEAR`, `MONTH`, transport type and trips. The collection is refreshed monthly and was updated in January 2026. [RTA trip dataset](https://gslb.dubaipulse.gov.ae/data/rta-public-transports/rta_public_transport_trips_by_type_of_transport_month-open) | Import the CSVs; show mode/month trends; forecast the next 1–3 months per mode; explain peak/low periods. | Hold out the latest six months and report MAE/MAPE against a seasonal-naive baseline. Also test whether the importer accepts every published file. | **Low.** Keep it aggregate; do not build a live route planner or claim operational forecasts. |
| **2. Dubai food catalogue search and label explainer** | Dubai Municipality publishes the Food Item Catalogue as a downloadable CSV, updated monthly, with product description, origin, ingredients, package details and availability/compliance fields. [Dubai Municipality catalogue](https://gslb.dubaipulse.gov.ae/data/dm-general/dm_food_item_catalogue-open) | Arabic/English keyword search; filters for origin and packaging; highlight literal ingredient/allergen keywords and show the source record. | Create 30–50 labelled search queries and measure Precision@5; verify displayed fields against sampled CSV rows. | **Low–medium.** The data file is large; pre-index only the fields needed. Use a prominent “check the package; not medical advice” notice and never infer allergen safety. |
| **3. Road-safety analytics and campaign recommender** | The Ministry of Interior dataset covers accident counts by cause, emirate and year through 2024 under CC BY 4.0; RAK Police also publishes current downloadable traffic files. [MOI/FCSC accident-by-cause dataset](https://opendata.fcsc.gov.ae/%40ministry-interior/traffic-accidents-according-cause-accident), [RAK Police open data](https://www.rakpolice.gov.ae/OpenData) | A dashboard of causes and trends by emirate, with transparent rule-based campaign suggestions (for example, target the largest rising cause). | Reproduce published totals from raw data; back-test whether a recommendation made using year *t* selects a leading cause in *t+1*. | **Low.** Do not claim crash prediction, identify locations/people, or portray suggestions as official enforcement advice. |
| **4. Dubai multimodal commute coverage explorer** | RTA provides an official GTFS schedule/geographic feed, updated daily, plus a public-transport-stations API description. [RTA GTFS feed](https://www.gslb.dubaipulse.gov.ae/data/rta-public-transports/rta_gtfs-open), [station API](https://gslb.dubaipulse.gov.ae/data/rta-registers/rta_public_transportation_stations-open-api) | Offline GTFS map: nearest stops, service hours and transfer counts between selected stations; compare two simple route choices. | Validate parsed stop/trip counts and a fixed set of known station-to-station journeys against the source schedule. | **Medium.** Dubai Pulse credentials may be required for live APIs; make the submitted version work from a downloaded GTFS snapshot. Avoid accessibility claims unless the feed explicitly provides the attribute. |
| **5. Dubai food-business discovery and data-quality map** | Dubai Municipality publishes food-business information derived from FoodWatch, including GPS coordinates, activities, employee count, cuisine and licensing agency; it is refreshed monthly. [Food-business dataset](https://gslb.dubaipulse.gov.ae/data/dm-inspections/dm_food_business_information-open) | Filterable map and directory with cuisine/activity/search; flag missing coordinates or duplicate-looking records for data-quality review. | Validate geospatial bounds, duplicate rate, and filter results on a labelled sample. | **Low.** Do not invent inspection scores or food-safety ratings that the dataset does not contain. |
| **6. Dubai household utility-use learning calculator** | DEWA publishes its tariff calculator and slab-tariff information, and provides official consumption-management tools. [DEWA tariff calculator](https://www.dewa.gov.ae/en/consumer/billing/tariff-calculator), [DEWA slab tariff](https://www.dewa.gov.ae/en/consumer/billing/slab-tariff) | User enters monthly electricity/water consumption; app estimates the bill and shows which slabs are crossed, then offers conservation scenarios. | Compare 20 representative inputs with DEWA's calculator; require exact rounding agreement or document any tariff/version difference. | **Medium.** Tariffs can change and calculator logic may include fees; label it an educational estimator, version the tariff table, and do not request DEWA accounts/bills. |
| **7. UAE waste-trend explorer** | The federal open-data portal offers waste statistics by emirate, type and amount, and describes its CC BY 4.0 licence; UAE.Stat also exposes national waste series through 2024. [FCSC waste dataset](https://opendata.fcsc.gov.ae/dataset/waste-statistics), [UAE.Stat](https://uaestat.fcsc.gov.ae/en) | Interactive comparisons of waste mix by emirate/year and a simple “what changed?” narrative generated from the data. | Reconcile chart totals to source records and test each narrative statement against its calculated value. | **Low.** The detailed FCSC dataset is older (2009–2015), so it is a historical-analysis project, not a real-time recycling adviser. |
| **8. UAE labour-market trend dashboard** | FCSC publishes employment by age and gender through 2024 with CC BY 4.0 licensing, while UAE.Stat publishes labour-force series. [Employment dataset](https://opendata.fcsc.gov.ae/dataset/data-on-employment-in-the-united-arab-emirates-2010-2022), [UAE.Stat labour-force catalogue](https://uaestat.fcsc.gov.ae/en) | Explore demographic trends, compare annual changes, and export a transparent evidence card for a selected segment. | Check derived growth rates against hand-calculated fixtures and validate filters against raw records. | **Low.** Do not turn aggregate figures into job recommendations or make causal claims about groups. |
| **9. UAE emissions and sustainability data story** | The Ministry of Climate Change and Environment's open dataset reports UAE greenhouse-gas emissions by gas and use type for 2013–2021, under CC BY 4.0. [MOCCAE emissions dataset](https://opendata.fcsc.gov.ae/dataset/b78880c7-b0d5-4099-8b96-efd5b7b4fa1c) | A visual explorer that decomposes trends by gas/use type and lets students generate a source-linked one-page data story. | Reproduce totals and percentage shares from raw records; review each generated statement for source/number consistency. | **Low.** Data ends in 2021; frame it as historical public-data literacy, not a current national emissions inventory or carbon calculator. |

## Selection guidance

Choose **1** if the course rewards data engineering/forecasting; choose **2** if it rewards a polished user-facing application; choose **3** if it rewards a civic UAE problem with rigorous, explainable analysis. All three can be completed with a local data snapshot, so a portal outage or an API key does not block the demo.

## Source notes

- Dubai Pulse is Digital Dubai's official data platform. Dataset pages above state update frequency, fields/resources and the publisher; download a dated snapshot and include its source URL in the project README.
- FCSC's Open Data UAE pages identify the producing authority, coverage and CC BY 4.0 licence. Respect those terms and cite the dataset in the presentation.
- Official sources establish data availability and policy context; they do **not** validate a student application's output. Evaluation must therefore be against held-out source data, known records, or the matching official calculator as specified above.
