# Source and claim ledger: UAE CV product opportunity screen

**Audience:** student selecting a self-serve, deployable UAE computer-vision product.  
**Research date:** 4 September 2026.  
**Decision criteria:** public/licensable development data; user images allowed only as runtime input; UAE relevance; computer vision central to value; generative AI constrained to evidence-backed writing; no compliance, safety, medical, insurance or legal verdicts.

| Claim | Evidence | Confidence and constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Dubai has a current, unusually substantial UAE food-product catalogue. | [Dubai Municipality Food Item Catalogue](https://www.gslb.dubaipulse.gov.ae/data/dm-general/dm_food_item_catalogue-open), labelled Open Data, is a 230.52 MB CSV, updated 26 January 2026 from the food-item registration service; metadata lists ingredients, origin, packaging and weight, source update daily and portal update monthly. | High for availability and fields. Verify access/API terms and stable identifiers before designing matching around it. |
| An openly licensed Arabic visual-OCR starting dataset exists. | [Humans in the Loop Arabic Documents OCR Dataset](https://humansintheloop.org/resources/datasets/arabic-documents-ocr-dataset/) has 10k images, including labels, with text boxes/transcriptions and CC0 1.0 dedication. | High for listed rights. Lebanese documents are not proof of UAE retail-label accuracy. |
| Arabic product information has UAE policy relevance, but a product should not make compliance verdicts. | [UAE Consumer Protection Law 15 of 2020](https://www.moet.gov.ae/documents/20121/0/Law_15_2020_pdf/b676fd26-275c-3652-e949-8b3663e7bd79) includes Arabic consumer-statement/e-commerce requirements; [ADAFSA general food-labelling practice](https://www.adafsa.gov.ae/CMS/Guidelines/Code%20of%20Practice%20No.%20%2805%29%20of%202019%20General%20Food%20Labelling%20Requirements.pdf) sets Arabic mandatory-information requirements. | High for policy context; exact application depends on product/category/jurisdiction. |

A CV report cannot certify accessibility. |
| A large permissive accessibility-navigation corpus is available. | [Google SANPO](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/sanpo_dataset) documents real/synthetic accessibility-navigation data under CC BY 4.0. | High on owner-stated source terms; its scale and non-UAE domain mean it is a development/benchmark input, not local validation. |

It does not validate visual defect detection or any performance conclusion. |

Obtain legal advice before processing staff, customers or sensitive data at scale. |

## Research stop rationale

Three independent research lanes were reconciled with direct source checks. Food-label evidence reconciliation initially ranked well on data alone, but was removed after the consumer-substitute screen: barcode lookup is simpler, and the differentiated supplier-to-retailer version requires private partner data. Accessibility, solar evidence, map-scale change and dashcam coaching remain viable, but no option has yet passed the full user-need and IP screen.
