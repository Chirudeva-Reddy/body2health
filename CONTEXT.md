# Product opportunity glossary

## Public self-serve product

A deployable application whose first release can be used by an individual or a small business without a pre-secured enterprise, government partner, proprietary data feed, or bespoke integration.

It may later serve organisations, but their workflow cannot be an MVP dependency.

## Public-source-only MVP

A first release whose model development and benchmark claims rest on regularly available, legally reusable public or open sources. The team must not depend on collecting or labelling a local training dataset.

User-contributed images or video may be normal runtime input when the product is useful without retaining or repurposing that material as a training dataset.

## Map-scale evidence

The product may identify dated, area-level land-cover or surface change from satellite imagery. It does not identify small objects, individual property defects, causes of change, legal status, safety, value, or environmental impact without separate authorised evidence.

## Evidence report

A result containing observed visual evidence, source or capture provenance, confidence, and an explicit unavailable or unreadable state. It may propose a low-stakes inspection next step, but never makes a compliance, legal, medical, safety, insurance, financial, or enforcement determination.

Generative AI may translate or organise an evidence report only from structured, source-linked findings; it cannot supply unobserved facts or decide the outcome.

## Automotive priority

Automotive opportunities are preferred, but a non-automotive direction may remain a serious project candidate when it has a stronger user problem, data foundation, deployment path and responsible-AI boundary.
